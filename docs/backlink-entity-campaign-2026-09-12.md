## Priority method

Scores are from 0–5. Higher is better for topical relevance (**R**), entity value (**E**), domain quality (**Q**), local relevance (**L**), likely indexability (**I**) and backlink/referral value (**B**). **Effort** and **Risk** are also 0–5, where higher means more effort or more risk. Priority score is:

`2R + 2E + Q + L + I + B - Effort - Risk`

Priority bands: **High** 24+, **Medium** 17–23, **Low** 16 or below. A high score does not override eligibility, payment or truthfulness constraints.
